Country flags are self-hosted 96×72 lossless WebP raster exports of the unchanged 4:3 SVG artwork from `flag-icons` 7.5.0 by lipis, under the bundled MIT licence. Small raster exports avoid sending large vector emblems for a 24-pixel interface icon.

Source: https://www.npmjs.com/package/flag-icons/v/7.5.0

The subset matches the 249 ISO entries in `delivery-countries.json`. Only the selected flag is loaded in the header; displaying a destination does not imply delivery availability.
